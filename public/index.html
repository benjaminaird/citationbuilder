CitationBuilder

A Marine Corps award drafting aid for turning rough achievements into a Summary of Action and proposed citation.

Drafting aid only: Verify formatting, opening/closing lines, character limits, and routing requirements with current command guidance, iAPS, SECNAV M-1650.1, and SECNAV M-5216.5.
CitationBuilder is not an official Department of Defense, Department of the Navy, or Marine Corps product.
